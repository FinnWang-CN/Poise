BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know.

Can I use Poise without a smart scale?

Yes. Enter weight, body fat and notes manually, or import your history from a Fitdays .xlsx export.

How do I turn on automatic sync?

Open Settings → Health data and tap Authorize and read once. Connected data then syncs when you open or return to the app. Turn it off at any time. Android currently reads up to the last 30 days; another app must have written measurements to the system.

How do I import Keep records?

Open Settings → Keep body journal. Poise checks for Keep and opens it directly for authorization, without a QR image. Install Keep and sign in first if missing. Confirm in Keep, then manually return to Poise. Reading opens a bottom sheet with a fixed confirmation button. Close without saving, or confirm to save and see a success dialog. Latest height is optional; Keep BMI is previewed separately. Values without a measurement time are marked Date only. Keep import is manual in this version and is available in native Android / iOS apps, not Web.

What should I do before switching phones?

Export a JSON backup in Settings and keep it safe, then import it on your new device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may erase local records.

Can these metrics be used for medical decisions?

Poise is for personal tracking and observing trends. Body fat comes from measurements or manual entries. BMI uses your current height. None of these are intended for medical diagnosis.
